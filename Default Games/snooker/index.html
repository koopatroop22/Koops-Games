THE RULES OF THE TABLE

Snooker, at a glance.

Build your break

Pot a red for 1 point, then a colour of your choice (yellow 2, green 3, brown 4, blue 5, pink 6 or black 7). A colour potted while reds remain is put back on its spot. Keep alternating red and colour while a red remains.

Clear the colours

After the final red, play one colour, then clear yellow, green, brown, blue, pink and black in order. These colours stay potted. The player with the most points wins the frame.

Fouls and snookers

It is a foul to miss the ball on, hit a ball out of order, pot the cue ball, or send a ball off the table. The other player receives at least 4 points (up to 7 for higher-value balls) and takes the next turn. A cue-ball scratch gives the incoming player ball in hand in the D.

After a foul, the incoming player can nominate a free ball when all balls on are snookered, or ask for a foul-and-miss replay. A nominated free ball counts as the ball on and is re-spotted if potted.

Frame tie and concession

If scores are level after the black, the black is re-spotted. The next pot or foul decides the frame. A player may concede a frame. A match is played to the selected best-of length.

Controls

Move the pointer across the table to aim. Use the power slider and select Take Shot. After a scratch, click inside the D to place the cue ball. Press Space to shoot.
