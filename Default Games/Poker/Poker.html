TEXAS HOLD'EM GUIDE

Rules & Hand Rankings

1. Blinds & Dealing: Before each hand, the seat left of the Dealer (D) posts the Small Blind ($10) and the next seat posts the Big Blind ($20). Every player receives 2 private hole cards.

2. Betting Streets: Four rounds of betting occur—Pre-Flop, Flop (3 shared cards), Turn (4th card), and River (5th card). You may Fold, Check (when no extra bet is required), Call, or Bet/Raise.

3. Showdown: Remaining players form the strongest 5-card poker hand using any combination of their 2 hole cards and the 5 community cards. Ties split the pot evenly.

1. Royal Flush — A K Q J 10 suited
2. Straight Flush — 5 sequential suited cards
3. Four of a Kind — 4 cards of equal rank
4. Full House — 3 of a kind + a pair
5. Flush — Any 5 cards of the same suit
6. Straight — 5 sequential mixed suits
7. Three of a Kind — 3 cards of equal rank
8. Two Pair — Two separate pairs
