28 November 2026 - New features & improvements

A bigger community and a more playable hub

Community lobbies now connect to real online rooms, every 3D table reacts to you, and Cue Sports now offers three playable modes.

  • +Community Area: host and browse Monopoly or Connect 4 lobbies, share room codes, and launch into the matching online room. Local preview lobbies no longer show made-up live room codes.
  • +Tower Defence 3D: build and upgrade four tower types, fight enemy waves with distinct abilities, earn reserves, and protect the Nexus across three maps.
  • +Terms before play: review and accept the Terms & Conditions before launching or directly opening a game. Poker keeps its separate 18+ free-play confirmation.
  • +Cue Sports 3D: choose snooker, 8-ball with solids and stripes, or American 9-ball, each with its own rack, rules, fouls, and win conditions.
  • +Living 3D tables: hover or click game tables to see dice roll, chess play one of five random openings, ships rock, pool balls break, tokens drop, mines reveal, and cards deal.
Your Terms acceptance is remembered in this browser. Read the full Terms & Conditions at any time.

Bring your friends to the table

Invite friends into online rooms with shareable links, so it’s easier to get a match started.

27 November 2026 · Multiplayer reliability

More reliable shared games

Online Monopoly rooms now track state updates with player identity, alongside clearer access rules for room members.

  • ✦Improved synchronization and identification of room updates.
  • ✦Refined room access so players can participate in their own match.
27 November 2026 · Fixes & polish

Smoother room setup

We improved online room creation and joining so it’s easier to understand what’s happening when you connect.

  • ✦Added clearer feedback for room creation and permission problems.
